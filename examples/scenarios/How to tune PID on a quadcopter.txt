# How to tune PID on a quadcopter: scenarios
Scenario 1
    Prepare
    To the motor sound
    Remember, PID tuning is subjective!
    Backup your current PID values
    Switch to Rate Mode (aka Acro Mode)
    Make sure your quadcopter’s CG (centre of gravity) is right in the middle
    Set initial values
    Set RC rates, super rates and expo
    Set default PID values
    Lower all the values by at least half
    P on Roll
    With good P, the control should feel precise
    Cruise around
    Do some sharp turns
    Does the quad dip to one side (like a wobble)? - No
    Do slow oscillations occur? - No
    Do fast oscilations occur? - No
    When P is right, you should get minimum oscillations when doing sharp turns.
    P on Pitch
    Do a split-S (where you move both the throttle and pitch sticks)
    Increase throttle to recover, pay attention to the pitch movement
    Does the quad pitch up more than it should? - No
    Do fast oscilations occur? - No
    Are the motors making a twitching sound? - No
    The quad feels very responsive and nimble
    TPA
    Do a punch out
    Does the quad get oscillations with high throttle? - No
    D on Roll and Pitch
    Do aggressive maneuvers like flips and rolls
    Does the quad overshoot at the end of the move then bounce back? - No
    Does prop wash happen? (oscillation when you descent) - No
    Do fast oscillation happen at the end of a roll or flip? - No
    Note that to eliminate bounces at the end of a flip or roll, you can also use Setpoint Transition
    I on Roll
    Bank your quad to the left and right
    Release stick
    Is the quad holding the angle well? - Yes
    Does the quad have stiff, robotic feeling? - No
    Does the quad have oscilations? - No
    You might want to increase I a bit more on a windy day
    I on Pitch
    Fly forward, do not increase throttle
    Does the nose keep the same angle? - Yes
    Anti Gravity Gain
    Fly in a straight line
    Do some rapid punchouts
    Does pitch stay at the same angle? - Yes
    3 to 5 is a good range
    P on Yaw
    Spin quickly on the yaw axis
    Stop spinning
    Does the quad dip one side? - No
    Do you get fast oscilations? - No
    When Yaw P is right the spin should be clean and snappy
    I on Yaw
    Fly straight
    Make a turn
    Is there drift, does the quad slide out? - No
    Yaw I should not be higher than just enough to prevent drift.
